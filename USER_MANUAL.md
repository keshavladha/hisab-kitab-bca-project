# FinPulse End-User Manual & Operating Guide

Welcome to **FinPulse**! This guide assists users in managing their personal finances, tracking income and expenses, setting budget caps, and viewing financial analytics.

---

## 1. Landing Page Navigation
When you open `http://localhost:3001`, you land on the Public Landing Page featuring an overview of key financial metrics and platform features.

---

## 2. Dashboard Overview
Click **Open Live Dashboard** or use the left navigation bar to access the Dashboard.
- **Top Metrics Cards**: View Total Balance, Monthly Income, Monthly Expenses, and Net Savings Rate.
- **Trend Charts**: Interactive line/bar charts showing 6-month comparisons.
- **Recent Transactions Table**: Quick preview of your latest 5 logged financial entries.

---

## 3. Adding Transactions (Income & Expenses)
1. Click the **+ Add Transaction** button in the top navigation header.
2. Enter the transaction title (e.g., "Salary", "Groceries").
3. Select amount, type (Income/Expense), category, date, and optional note.
4. Click **Save Transaction**. The dashboard stats and charts update immediately!

---

## 4. Managing Budgets
Navigate to **Budget Planner** from the sidebar menu to view your category spending caps.
- **Green Progress Bar**: On Track (<80% spent)
- **Yellow Progress Bar**: Warning Threshold (>=80% spent)
- **Red Progress Bar**: Over Budget (>100% spent)

---

## 5. Exporting Data
Navigate to **Transaction History** or **Settings**:
- Click **Export CSV** to download a spreadsheet file of all transactions.
- Click **Export JSON** to save a complete database backup.
